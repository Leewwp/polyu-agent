理大與哈爾濱工業大學共同成立深圳國際設計學院及未來設計聯合研究中心

香港理工大學（理大）與哈爾濱工業大學（哈工大）於9月24日正式成立「哈爾濱工業大學香港理工大學深圳國際設計學院」（學院），並同步啟動「未來設計聯合研究中心」（中心），標誌兩校於教育、科研及創新領域的合作邁向新里程。揭牌儀式於哈工大深圳校區舉行，由理大校長滕錦光院士及哈工大黨委常委、校長韓傑才院士等雙方嘉賓共同見證。  學院獲國家教育部批准成立，結合理大在設計教育與研究方面的國際領先優勢，以及哈工大在工程科技領域的卓越實力，致力培育學生成爲具跨學科視野、創新思維及科技素養的未來人才。 滕錦光院士表示：「這次合作建基於兩校高度契合的辦學理念和發展願景，體現服務國家發展、推動教育創新和培育未來人才的共同使命。兩大平台不但將成為兩校深化協同創新、推動產學研深度融合的重要載體，亦能為深化大灣區教育科研合作、服務大灣區建設發揮示範引領作用。」 韓傑才院士表示：「兩校在合作辦學、共建實驗室及博士生培養等領域有著良好的合作基礎。希望雙方充分發揮各自優勢，攜手推進學院建設，加強工程技術與設計創新的交叉融合，培養具有設計思維的複合型人才，為學校邁向世界領先大學注入新動力。」 學院將於2027/28學年在哈工大深圳校區開辦碩士研究生課程，涵蓋設計、智能科學與技術，以及城鄉規劃三個範疇。課程為期三年，首年計劃招收150名全日制碩士研究生。課程由兩校聯合統籌及授課，完成課程並符合畢業要求的學生，將由兩校分別頒發學位證書。  此外，兩校亦同步啟動「未來設計聯合研究中心」，聚焦設計、科技與社會融合發展的跨學科研究，重點推進未來居所與智慧城市、系統整合設計，以及文化、藝術及數字媒體科技三大前沿研究方向，促進創新研究、知識轉移及科研成果轉化，為國家創新驅動發展及大灣區建設注入新動能。  揭牌儀式後，兩校隨即召開首屆聯合管理委員會第一次會議，審議學院治理架構、學術委員會組成及研究生招生方案等重要事項，為學院未來發展奠定堅實基礎。

Call for Submissions | 2026 19th PhD Colloquium on Design Research

The 2026 19th PhD Colloquium on Design Research will be held at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University on 10 and 12 November 2026 and hosted by its School of Design.   Under the theme “Building Translational Design Knowledge”, the Colloquium will bring together design scholars, faculty members, and doctoral researchers from institutions in Hong Kong, Mainland China, and overseas to explore emerging directions in design research across four areas: Design Intelligence, Design Wellbeings, Design for Virtue, and Translational Knowledge.   Doctoral students in design and related disciplines worldwide who have passed their confirmation of candidature are invited to submit a research abstract with their supervisor’s recommendation. Outstanding applicants will receive a formal invitation to present their research and showcase a poster at the Colloquium, with feedback from experts across participating institutions. Best Presentation and Best Poster Awards will be presented.    Submission deadline: 24:00, 30 September 2026 (Beijing Time)   Please refer to the Call for Submissions for the submission procedure, email address, and full requirements.   We warmly invite doctoral researchers to submit their work and welcome colleagues and partner institutions to share this call with their academic communities.     Organiser The Hong Kong Polytechnic University School of Design Supporting Institutions Tsinghua University Tongji University Jiangnan University Hunan University National Cheng Kung University Guangdong University of Technology

Results Announcement for Selected Projects for LKSD&I Capstone Project Funding Scheme 2025/26

PolyU Li Ka Shing Design and Innovation Centre (LKSD&I) has successfully completed Judging Session on 3 August 2026 for LKSD&I Capstone Project Funding Scheme 2025/26, a funding scheme that encourages students to transform their capstone projects into real ventures, establish startups, and pursue further funding through the PolyVentures Ecosystem. This year, LKSD&I has received 20+ nominations from different design disciplines, and there are 8 projects have been selected by the judging panel, comprising of Prof. Kun-Pyo Lee, Dean of School of Design; Prof. Henry Duh, Associate Dean (Global and Industry Engagement); Prof. Benny Leong, Professor of Practice; and Ms Winnie Cheng, Manager of PolyU Knowledge Transfer and Entrepreneurship Office (KTEO. Selected projects demonstrated outstanding performance in four core aspects among all received project applications: (1) Innovativeness and Originality, (2) Business Development Potential, (3) Research and Development, and (4) Booth Presentation at PolyU Design Show 2026. List of selected projects are indicated as below in alphabetical order:  Selected Projects for LKSD&I Capstone Project Funding Scheme 2025/26 Integrated Designpreneurship (InD) Project #01 Project Name: City Sense Diary Project Team Member: Lai Wai Shan Project Description: City Sense Diary is a sensory mobility platform designed for visually impaired people to support free exploration, reflection, and social connection beyond point-to-point navigation. Users can record sensory notes during their journeys, revisit them as diaries, share experiences with others, and join short co-walking trips. Project #02 Project Name: SEW+ Project Team Member: Yeung Wan Chiu, Ma Yicheng Project Description: Hong Kong’s female labour has the lowest rate among most Asian cities where childcare and inflexible work conditions are the primary barriers. Su Embroidery is at a risk of generational extinction within a decade. The project turns an endangered skill into a flexible earning and livelihood for the women who need it most. Project #03 Project Name: THE RUINED CITY: Scavenger 0 Project Team Member: Peng Zixun Project Description: The Ruined City: Scavenger 0 is a low-tech, mixed-media narrative game project that helps people re-reading everyday life through overlooked or functionless objects. It responds to a task-driven lifestyle in which people often know what needs to be done next but may lose sensitivity to how their own condition is changing. Product Design (PRD) Project #04 Project Name: HI-TOUCH — Connection in Motion Project Team Member: Chen Ching Lam Project Description: HI-TOUCH addresses the lack of engaging, socially interactive park facilities for young people. It responds to the need for a more usable and inclusive public space that encourages activity, connection, and a stronger relationship with nature. MDes — Smart Service Design (SSD) Project #05 Project Name: Weaving Identity Through Friendship Project Team Member: ZHANG Hanmei, LIU Yinghon, FAN Shuyu, ZHANG Kaiying, QU Sichen Project Description: WEAVE is a friendship-based digital service that helps Gen Z women exploring their own identity through meaningful interaction with close friends through the WEAVE platform. MSc in Innovative Multimedia Entertainment (MScIME) Project #06 Project Name: BeeCare: The AI-Driven Digitalization of Welfare Workflows Project Team Member: Song Zhixin Project Description: BeeCare is a mobile workflow platform designed to reduce administrative burden on frontline social workers in Hong Kong's elderly care sector. Social workers' time can be transformed into direct care, reducing risks of severe burnout, and alleviating the sector's high turnover rate. Project #07 Project Name: EcceHomo: The Last Lucid Dream Project Team Member: Tang Jian, Gong Yunfei, Li Zhi, Yu Fan Project Description: Current humanities education and museum exhibitions suffer from an empathy deficit, highly relying on passive observation rather than active participation. High-fidelity cultural VR experiences face prohibitive custom development costs. The project makes scale and replication more feasible for standard cultural institutions. Project #08 Project Name: SensePlay Project Team Member: Cho Ka Yan, Siu Yat Long, Wong Ngo Fung Project Description: SensePlay alleviates challenges faced by students with moderate to severe Special Educational Needs. The tools are designed for their complex sensory, physical, and cognitive needs, while assisting therapists to deliver personalised support through real-time progress tracking, customisation, and system integration in schools and service centres. Each selected project teams will receive HK$15,000 on a conditional offer from LKSD&I, subject to their status of meeting the conditional offer requirements. These include: (1) Applied to the PolyU Micro Fund 2025/26 (Cohort 2) and selected as one of the shortlisted teams / top performers or Will apply to PolyU Micro Fund 2026/27 (Cohort 1); and (2) Establishing a company in Hong Kong within 6 months after the announcement from LKSD&I, SD. The Principal Applicant serving as the company director and hold at least 50% of the shares or alternatively hold the largest shareholding among all company directors. Selected teams will be notified and shared with further information in the logistic arrangements. Once again, we would like to congratulate all selected project teams that successfully granted with the funding from the funding scheme. And we would like to take the opportunity to thank for all the contributions and supports from all staff members and students from PolyU. LKSD&I will continue working closely to empower and nurture PolyU Design students with unleashed talents in design, innovation, and entrepreneurship. For further updates on the Capstone Project Funding Scheme 2026/27, please stay tuned and details will be announced shortly on the LKSD&I webpage on the PolyU Design website. With the generous support of the Li Ka Shing Foundation, LKSD&I is dedicated to empowering PolyU Design students by nurturing their talents in design, innovation, and entrepreneurship.

Tsang Hoi Yi Neme's Capstone Project MUSEC Recognised as James Dyson Award 2026 Hong Kong Runner-up

TSANG Hoi Yi Neme, a recent graduate of the BA (Hons) in Design (Product Design) programme at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University School of Design (PolyU Design), has been named a Hong Kong Runner-up in the James Dyson Award 2026 for MUSEC Arm Sleeves, an innovative wearable music interaction device developed as her final-year capstone project. Developed as a final-year capstone project, MUSEC is a wearable music interaction device that transforms body movement into sound and haptic feedback. Combining gesture recognition, touch controls and a companion mobile app, it enables users to create and personalise music through physical interaction. Featuring both Casual Mode for everyday use and Dancer Mode for expressive performance, the project reimagines music listening as an active, immersive and embodied experience. This breakthrough design offers music lovers and dancers a more engaging and physical way to experience sound. Created under the supervision of Stephen J. Wang, MUSEC reflects PolyU Design's commitment to nurturing future designers through interdisciplinary education, creative inquiry and practice-based learning. By integrating wearable technology, interaction design and user experience, the project demonstrates how students are empowered to develop innovative design solutions that respond to emerging opportunities and contemporary needs. The James Dyson Award is an international design and engineering competition that celebrates problem-solving inventions by emerging designers and engineers. Following its recognition as a Hong Kong Runner-up, MUSEC will advance to the next stage of the competition alongside the other awarded projects from Hong Kong. The international Top 20 shortlist will be announced in October and selected by a global team of Dyson engineers, while the global winners will be announced in November and chosen by Sir James Dyson.   Learn more about MUSEC

PolyU Design and St. Petersburg State University Sign MoU to Strengthen Design and Creative Economy Collaboration

The Hong Kong Polytechnic University School of Design (PolyU Design) recently welcomed a delegation from St. Petersburg State University (SPbU) for the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU), establishing a framework for collaboration in joint educational initiatives, academic exchange, co-developed research, student exchange, and work-integrated education opportunities. The signing builds on SPbU's visit to PolyU Design in December last year and marks an important step in strengthening international partnerships in design, innovation and the creative economy. The MOU was signed by Prof. Kun-Pyo Lee, Dean of PolyU Design, and Prof. Anastasia Yarmosh, First Vice Rector for Academic Affairs of SPbU. Witnessing the signing were Prof. Henry Duh, Associate Dean (Global & Industry Engagement), Prof. Jaehyun Jaden Park, Associate Professor, and Prof. Shera Hyunyim Park, Assistant Professor, and Ms Emily Ng, Manager of PolyU Design, alongside Prof. Sergey Andryushin, Vice Rector for International Affairs, Mr. Danil Tolmachev, Director of the School of Creative Economy, and Ms Anna Tomchik, Deputy Director of the School of Creative Economy of SPbU. A key area of common interest is the development of the creative economy, an emerging field that brings together design, innovative business, AI and cultural technologies. Through the new partnership, PolyU Design and SPbU will explore how design, particularly in the areas of service design and digital solutions, can contribute to the development of creative cities and future industries. The collaboration further extends PolyU Design's global network of academic collaborators and supports Hong Kong's ongoing efforts to strengthen its position as an international education hub, foster talent cultivation, and expand innovation partnerships. Looking ahead, PolyU Design looks forward to welcoming students and colleagues from SPbU to Hong Kong in 2027 and beyond for exchanges, collaborative projects and academic activities, further strengthening ties between the two institutions and opening new opportunities for learning, research and innovation.

PolyU Design Announces Strategic AI Partnership with Alibaba International Design and HKDA

The Hong Kong Polytechnic University School of Design (PolyU Design) has taken a step forward in advancing design education and innovation through a strategic partnership with Alibaba International Design and the Hong Kong Designers Association (HKDA). Formalised through a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) signed during the Hong Kong Design Summit 2026, the collaboration will explore new opportunities in AI-driven design education, industry-academic collaboration, and innovation research, while contributing to the growth of Hong Kong and the Greater Bay Area's creative industries. The MOU brings together the expertise of academia, industry and the professional design community to address emerging opportunities at the intersection of design and artificial intelligence. Key areas of collaboration include the joint development and delivery of academic and professional training programmes, industry-academia partnerships that foster design and technology innovation, and the co-organisation of flagship events, forums and summits that promote design excellence and cross-sector exchange. AI-related courses have been identified as the initial focus of programme collaboration. The agreement was signed by Prof. Kun-Pyo Lee, Dean of PolyU Design, Mr Gu Wei Head of AIGC Design Business at Alibaba International Design, and Ms Mui Kinoshita, Chairman of HKDA, during the summit attended by leaders from the creative, business and education sectors. Witnessing the signing were Prof. Henry Duh, Associate Dean (Global & Industry Engagement) of PolyU Design, alongside representatives from Alibaba International Design and HKDA. As one of world's leading design schools, PolyU Design has long championed interdisciplinary learning and close engagement with industry. The new partnership reflects the School's commitment to preparing future designers for a rapidly evolving professional landscape shaped by emerging technologies and new modes of creative practice. By integrating AI into design education and fostering stronger industry connections, PolyU Design seeks to equip students and professionals with the skills and perspectives needed to drive meaningful innovation. The collaboration also aligns with broader efforts to strengthen Hong Kong's position as a regional hub for design, technology and creative entrepreneurship. Through joint initiatives spanning education, research and professional development, the three partners aim to create opportunities for knowledge exchange, talent cultivation and innovation across Hong Kong and the Greater Bay Area. The MOU signing was one of the highlights of the Hong Kong Design Summit 2026, an annual gathering of designers, industry leaders and innovators organised by HKDA. The summit featured business matching activities, industry showcases and discussions on the future of design, emphasising the growing importance of collaboration between academia and industry in driving creative and economic development. The partnership underscores the School's ongoing mission to challenge conventions, embrace new technologies and explore innovative approaches to design education and research. By connecting creativity, technology and industry expertise, PolyU Design, Alibaba International Design and HKDA are working together to empower the next generation of designers and shape the future of the creative economy.

PolyU Design Partners with K11 by AC to Strengthen Industry-Academic Collaboration and Nurture Future Design Talent

The School of Design at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU Design) has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with K11 by AC Limited, marking a new strategic partnership to enhance industry-academic collaboration and create meaningful learning opportunities for future designers. The MOU signing ceremony was held at the K11 MUSEA Gentry Club, bringing together representatives from both organisations to formalise a shared commitment to nurturing emerging creative talent through industry engagement, experiential learning, and knowledge exchange. Under the partnership, PolyU Design students will benefit from a range of opportunities designed to bridge classroom learning and professional practice. These include internship placements with workplace supervision, collaborative projects that support academic and co-operative learning, and a series of design training workshops, professional talks, and knowledge-sharing activities jointly organised by PolyU Design and K11 by AC. Professor Lee Kun-pyo, Dean of PolyU Design, represented the School at the signing ceremony alongside Ms Maria Hui, Group Chief Human Resources Officer of K11 by AC. As one of Asia's leading design schools, PolyU Design is committed to preparing future designers to address real-world challenges through creativity, innovation, and interdisciplinary collaboration. This partnership with K11 by AC reflects the School's ongoing efforts to strengthen links with industry and provide students with valuable exposure to professional practice. Through this collaboration, PolyU Design and K11 by AC will work together to cultivate design talent, foster industry engagement, and contribute to the continued development of Hong Kong's creative ecosystem.

Finalist teams advanced through a competitive first round, participating in an intensive one-day Hatch Programme workshop where they received expert guidance to refine their design proposals and develop functional prototypes before pitching live to the judging panel.  The final presentation showcased extraordinary creativity, technical ingenuity, and practical application, impressing the jury
